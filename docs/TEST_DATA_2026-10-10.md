> credentials per society (Urban Heights #1 and Royal Eden #6 both have WhatsApp/SMS enabled).
> The mail layer applies a society's own SMTP settings over `MAIL_MAILER` at send time, so

> welcome email to a reserved `example.test` address was attempted through Royal Eden's stored
> SMTP login (it failed authentication; nothing was delivered). `EXTERNAL_MESSAGING_ENABLED=false`
> now closes this (`config/messaging.php`); every seeding/QA run after that used it, on copies
> with the credentials blanked. **Consider rotating the credentials stored in this database.**
