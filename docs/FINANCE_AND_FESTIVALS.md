# Finance, rent mapping and festivals (2026-10-16)

## Rent record on the wrong villa (159 vs 160)

Read-only diagnosis of the real DB (nothing was written):

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Rent bill | id 45, `RENT-6-123-202609-1790072554`, Sept 2026, Rs 10,000, pending, created 2026-09-22 10:22:34 |
| Bill's unit | flat **123** = Villa **160** ("Suman Sadan"), owner Suraj suman (owner 19) |
| Active tenant on flat 123 | none |
| Tenant | id 10 "Piyus chudarri" is on flat **131** = Villa **159** (tenant row last changed 2026-10-03 08:28:51) |
| History | no audit row ever moved the bill; the bill number encodes flat 123 from creation |

Root cause (code): `Admin\RentController::edit` listed only units with an active tenant, so a rent whose unit had lost its
tenant (tenancy moved with `UserTenantAssignmentController`, which never touches bills) had **no pre-selected option**; the
browser forced another pick and `update()` trusted the posted `flat_id` blindly: no tenant check, no duplicate-month check, no
audit, and a validation scoped to the caller's society. Edits could therefore silently re-point a rent. I could not prove from
the data that a previous manual correction was submitted and reverted: no edit was audited, and the bill still names flat 123.

Fix: the rent's own unit is always an option and pre-selected; omitting or repeating the unit changes nothing; moving a rent is a
deliberate, validated act (unit of the rent's own society, active tenant, no rent bill that month, **refused once payments exist**)
and writes a `rent.unit_changed` audit row. Tests: `tests/Feature/RentUnitMappingTest.php`.

**Correction of bill 45 (needs your authorisation and evidence):** confirm from the tenancy agreement/receipt that the Sept 2026
rent is for Villa 159, then Rent Management -> Edit rent 45 -> choose "159 - Garden (Piyus chudarri)" -> Save. The bill has no
payments (Payment 42 was deleted; do not restore it), so the move is allowed and audited. Do not bulk-reassign other rents; the
other two rent bills (society 1) are on units that still have an active tenant.

## Festival & Celebrations

Plan: reuse Events (announcement), roles, society scoping, ActivityLog. The festival fund is **separate**: contributions are not
maintenance income and festival spending is not a society expense, so nothing here reaches the society Financial Report (no double
counting) - covered by a test. Migration `2026_10_16_000001_create_festival_tables.php` (additive, reversible).

- Roles: Admin plans/approves/settles; Accountant records receipts, refunds and pays; committee members (assigned per festival)
  enter expenses and may approve up to the festival's approval limit (none set = Admin only); residents see published festivals
  and pledge. Staff/Guard have no access.
- Contributions: pledge (not money, no receipt) -> `recordReceipt` (verified; receipt `FCR...` issued; reference unique per society;
  non-cash needs a reference; no future date; idempotent by pledge and by form token) -> partial/full refund (never above received).
  No auto-debit, default amount, penalty or arrears; individual amounts visible only to Admin/Accountant (residents see own + totals).
- Expenses: draft -> submitted -> approved|rejected -> paid; cancel before paid. Approved = commitment; fund reduces only when paid;
  cannot pay beyond the fund; creator cannot approve (switchable per festival); budget-line limits enforced; duplicate guard.
- Settlement: needs no draft/submitted/approved-unpaid expenses and a recorded decision on any surplus/shortfall; then read-only.
  Report: HTML, PDF, CSV with audit trail. Carry-forward only via a documented verified opening balance (note required).
- Not built (honestly): payment gateway collection (project has none), mobile API/Flutter screens, festival-specific e-mail/SMS/
  WhatsApp templates (announcement goes through Events only), per-festival notification of payments. Tests: `FestivalModuleTest` (29).

## Annual report and future dates

The annual table now marks months that have not begun ("future - no data yet") and the running month ("in progress"), and the report flags receipts or
expenses already dated after today. They are **not** hidden or moved: each stays in the period of its own date, so months still add up to the year.
